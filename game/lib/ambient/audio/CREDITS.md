`tools/fetch_ambient_assets.py audio` (license-filtered to Creative Commons 0,
preview-quality OGG — ample for ambience). The two `_2` variants have no fetch
entry and stay synth placeholders (`tools/synth_ambience.py`, authored, treat
as CC0).
