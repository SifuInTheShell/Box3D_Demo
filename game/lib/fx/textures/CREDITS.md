# Texture credits

Particle sprites (this directory) come from Kenney's **Particle Pack**
(https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack), license **Creative Commons CC0**
(public domain, no attribution required — credited here anyway because
Kenney is a treasure).

Model credits (tree, props, character, the C4 charge, the derby cars, the sky
HDRI) are itemized in `../models/CREDITS.md` — including the two CC-BY items
(the C4 charge and the CarConcept derby shell) and their required attribution.

The fire flipbook (`fire_flipbook.png`) is **"Flame02"** from **Unity Labs'
Free VFX image sequences & flipbooks**
(https://unity.com/blog/engine-platform/free-vfx-image-sequences-flipbooks),
license **Creative Commons CC0** (public domain, no attribution required —
credited anyway). Houdini-rendered flame, 16×4 = 64 frames; the original TGA's
straight alpha is kept and RGB zeroed only on fully-transparent pixels.
